# Reporting behind the image

All items are events that happened, or were first reported, on Friday 20 November 2026 in some time zone. Items dated 19 November (the Shenzhen leaders' summit, the ICJ Rohingya ruling, Anthropic's IPO day) are deliberately excluded; Friday's market reaction to that debut is included.

Key checks:

- Cyclone Akvan: India Meteorological Department bulletin and Odisha state reporting via Hindustan Times, India Today, The Hindu. Landfall near Gopalpur on the morning of 20 Nov; roughly 300,000 evacuated; 70–80 km/h winds gusting to 90; no major damage reported.
- Kyiv: AP, BBC, Euronews, FT. Overnight missile barrage, three bridges hit, casualties reported, strikes on transport and energy.
- COP31: DW, CNN, Channel News Asia, Eco-Business. Final scheduled day in Antalya; revised draft drops fossil-fuel transition language and the $120bn adaptation figure; talks expected to run over.
- Asian markets: Reuters, Al Jazeera, Bloomberg/Business Times. Taiex −6.2%, Nikkei −5.1%, Kospi −5.2%, Hang Seng −1.9% on Friday, after Anthropic's 19 Nov debut.
- BepiColombo: ESA, New Scientist, Scientific American. Eight-year, ~10bn km cruise; arrival phase at Mercury, orbit insertion scheduled 21 Nov.
- UK–EU summit: BBC, FT, Euronews. Youth mobility and food agreements signed in Brussels.
- Other briefs: Reuters, AP, BBC, AFP, IMD, DW and Al Jazeera coverage dated 20 Nov 2026.

All hero and thumbnail illustrations were generated locally from text-only prompts with no reference photograph, using `gambar` with `--turbo` (INT8): three `--ar 16:9` heroes (2688x1536) and ten `--ar 1:1` thumbnails (2048x2048). They were centre-cropped and downsampled, and the final artifact is a quality-92 JPEG stored under the required entry.png name; all text, numbers, map and pins remain original vector drawing. No third-party artwork or photographs are used.
